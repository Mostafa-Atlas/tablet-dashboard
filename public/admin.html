        How to get the private ICS URL
        1. Open Google Calendar on a PC → ⚙ Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar
        2. Copy the Secret address in iCal format (ends with .ics)
        3. Paste it above → Connect. Never share that link — anyone with it can read your calendar.
